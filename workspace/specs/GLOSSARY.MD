# Glossary & Vocabulary

This file is the canonical vocabulary source for ServIQ documentation. Use these terms consistently across domain, technical, and product-facing docs.

## How to Use This Glossary

- Prefer the canonical term listed here when writing new documentation.
- If an older document uses a different term, keep the older term only when preserving historical context or compatibility.
- When renaming a term, update this glossary and the main index pages at the same time.

## Platform and Tenancy

- **ServIQ** — the multi-tenant service operations platform.
- **Platform** — the shared SaaS layer that serves all tenants.
- **Tenant** — a logically isolated customer instance on the platform.
- **Service Provider** — the business that operates inside a tenant and uses ServIQ to deliver services. Prefer this term when describing the business itself.
- **Platform Administrator** — the person who manages the shared ServIQ platform.
- **Tenant Administrator** — the person who manages one Service Provider's configuration, users, and operations.
- **Dispatcher** — the person who coordinates jobs, assignments, and operational exceptions for a Service Provider.
- **Service Agent** — the field worker who performs the service work.
- **Customer** — the person or organization that requests and receives the service.

## Customer Portal and Support

- **Customer Portal** — the customer-facing app or website used for signup, browsing, booking, tracking, and self-service.
- **Customer Support** — the tenant-facing support workflow for handling cases, complaints, follow-ups, escalations, and issue resolution.
- **Service Request** — the request a customer submits to receive service. Some older docs still say Customer Request, but Service Request is the preferred canonical term.
- **Service Job** — the scheduled operational work item created from a customer request.
- **Assignment** — the act of allocating a service job to a service agent.
- **Field Execution** — the on-site execution phase where the service agent completes the service job.

## Service Agent Portal

- **Service Agent Portal** — the service-agent-facing app or mobile portal used to accept assignments, view jobs, upload photos, upload proof of payment, capture customer confirmation, and complete the job. This is a presentation channel, not a standalone bounded context.

## Finance and Accounting

- **Tenant revenue** — income generated by a tenant's service operations.
- **Platform fee** — the amount retained by the platform from tenant activity.
- **Refund** — money returned to a customer after a charge or payment event.
- **Proof of Payment** — an uploaded receipt, screenshot, transfer confirmation, or similar document used to show that a payment was made.
- **Chargeback** — a payment reversal initiated through the payment processor or card network.
- **Payout** — money transferred to a tenant after fees and adjustments.
- **Fund Distribution** — the process of splitting a payment or net payable amount into explicit recipient buckets such as platform share, Service Provider share, Service Agent share, and other configured recipients.
- **Settlement** — the final status of a financial transaction after payment processing and reconciliation.
- **Reconciliation** — the process of matching platform records with payment processor records and internal financial data.

## Document Processing and AI

- **OCR** — Optical Character Recognition; the process of extracting text from images, scans, photos, or screenshots.
- **LLM** — Large Language Model; an AI model that interprets text and can classify, normalize, summarize, or generate structured output.

## Architecture Vocabulary

- **Bounded Context** — a domain boundary with its own model, responsibilities, and language.
- **Port** — an abstraction for an external capability such as messaging, persistence, or observability.
- **Adapter** — a concrete implementation of a port.
- **CQRS** — Command Query Responsibility Segregation; a pattern that separates command and query responsibilities.
- **Event-Driven Architecture** — an architecture style that uses events to communicate changes between parts of the system.
- **Hexagonal Architecture** — an architecture style that keeps business logic isolated behind ports and adapters.
