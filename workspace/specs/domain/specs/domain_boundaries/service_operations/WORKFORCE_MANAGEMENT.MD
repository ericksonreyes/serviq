# Workforce Management

### Responsibility

**Who is capable of performing the work?**

Core concepts:

```text
Service Agent
Skills
Certifications
Availability
Service Areas
Workload
Capacity
```

Example:

```text
John
├── AC Repair
├── Refrigerator Repair
├── Antipolo
├── Cainta
└── Available
```

This context answers:

> "Can John perform this job?"

It does **not** answer:

> "Should John receive this job?"

That decision belongs to Assignment & Dispatch.
