# Service Agent

**Example:** John — Appliance Technician

John performs the actual work.

His mobile app or Service Agent Portal might show:

```text
Today's Jobs

09:00  Refrigerator Repair
       Alice Santos
       Antipolo

11:30  Washing Machine Repair
       Bob Cruz
       Cainta

14:00  AC Maintenance
       Carlos Reyes
       Marikina
```

John can:

- Accept/reject assignments
- View job details
- Navigate to the customer
- Change job status
- Add notes
- Upload photos
- Record parts/materials
- Upload proof of payment
- Capture customer confirmation
- Complete the job

John doesn't get to see or modify QuickFix's entire customer database or configuration.
