# Platform Administrator

**Example:** Sarah — ServIQ Operations Manager

Sarah works for the company operating ServIQ itself.

She can:

- Create and manage Service Providers (tenants)
- Approve or suspend businesses
- Manage platform-wide configuration
- Manage subscription plans
- View platform-wide metrics
- Review tenant income, platform fee income, and settlement status
- Manage platform users and permissions

She **doesn't normally manage individual service jobs**.

```text
Platform Administrator
        │
        ├── manages → Service Providers
        ├── manages → Platform configuration
        └── monitors → Platform-wide operations
```

## Onboarding
Platform administrators are typically internal employees of the company operating ServIQ.
